Privacy Policy

Nymora: Horoscopes & Tarot · Effective November 26, 2026

Nymora is made by Made by Two ("we", "us"). You don't need an account, and we never see your name, your birth details or your readings. This page explains what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

What stays on your device

Your name, gender, birth date, birth time and birth place, your chart, your settings and your tarot journal are saved only on your iPhone. Your horoscope readings come with the app and are shown from your device. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Finding your birth place

When you type your birth place, the app uses Apple's map search to suggest places. What you type is sent to Apple for this, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive it.

Tarot readings

To write a tarot reading, the app sends your question, the spread and the cards you drew to our server. The server passes them to an AI service (currently DeepSeek) that writes the reading, and sends the reading back to your phone. We don't send your name or birth details, and our server doesn't keep your question or the reading after it is returned.

To keep to the daily limit, the server counts readings by a random ID created on your device (or, for Nymora Pro, by your subscription's ID). These counters hold only the ID, the date and a number, and expire within days. The AI service processes your question under its own terms; please don't include names or other personal details in your questions.

Purchases

Nymora Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation of the subscription, which the app uses to unlock Pro and our server uses to confirm Pro tarot readings. We never receive your payment details.

Advertising

Nymora shows ads from Google AdMob to readers without Nymora Pro: ads that look like cards between readings, full-screen ads at some breaks, and an ad when you return to the app. Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about the ads you see and tap, and how you use the app
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads, and may personalise ads if you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Nymora asks for your consent first through Google's consent form. On every device you can turn off tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Nymora Pro subscribers see no ads.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Nymora schedules it on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

Nymora is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can change or delete your profile in the app, delete single tarot readings from your journal, and delete everything by deleting the app. Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side; for data held by Apple, Google or the AI service, use their privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
