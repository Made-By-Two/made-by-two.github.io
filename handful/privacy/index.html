Privacy Policy

Handful: Daily Word Puzzle · Effective November 22, 2026

Handful is made by Made by Two ("we", "us"). We don't run servers for Handful, we don't ask you to make an account, and we never see your name, email or game history. This page explains what does leave your device, and why.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your games, coins, streaks, statistics and settings are saved on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through Apple's iCloud key-value storage so they follow you to your other devices. That copy lives in your own iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we can't read it.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and streaks are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data; you can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Purchases

Handful Pro and coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, which the app uses to unlock what you bought. We never receive your payment details.

Advertising

Handful shows ads from Google AdMob to players who haven't bought Handful Pro: a short ad between some practice games, and optional ads you choose to watch for coins or an extra row. Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about the ads you see and tap, and how you interact with the app
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads, and may use it to personalise ads if you allow it. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Handful asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Handful under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On every device you can turn off tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Players with Handful Pro see no ads, and the ad software doesn't start for them.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Handful schedules them on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

Handful is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete all Handful data on your device by deleting the app, and reset your statistics from Settings in the app. Because we don't hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side; for data held by Apple or Google, use their privacy tools linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
