Common questions

When does the new puzzle come out?
At midnight in your time zone. Everyone plays the same word on the same date.
How do I get Handful Pro back on a new phone?
Open Handful, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
My coins or streak didn't move to my new device.
Handful syncs through iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud on both devices, then open Handful on both while online.
What does a streak freeze do?
If you miss a day, a freeze is used automatically so your streak continues. You can hold up to two; Pro players get one free each week.
I ran out of rows. Can I keep going?
Once per game you can add one more row for coins or by watching a short ad (free with Pro). Or reveal the answer and come back tomorrow.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Settings → Ad privacy choices in Handful. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
I was charged but didn't get my coins.
Reopen Handful while online; unfinished purchases are delivered when the app starts. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of purchase.
