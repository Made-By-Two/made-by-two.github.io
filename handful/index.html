Handful

Ten tiles. One word. Every day.

HANDS

Handful is a daily word puzzle for iPhone. You're dealt a hand of ten letter tiles and spell guesses only from what you hold. Each guess shows which letters are right, which are in the word somewhere, and which aren't in it at all. Draw new tiles, swap the ones that don't help, and find the word in six rows.

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Keep a streak going, compare scores on Game Center, and play practice games as often as you like.
