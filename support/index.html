Nymora: common questions

How do I get Nymora Pro back on a new phone?
Open Nymora, go to the Profile tab (the person icon), scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
How do I cancel or change my subscription?
In Nymora, Profile → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nymora. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
When can I ask the cards again?
Without Pro you get one tarot reading a day. When it's used, Nymora shows the time your next reading opens. Nymora Pro removes the daily limit.
I don't know my birth time.
That's fine: choose “I don't know my birth time”. Nymora then works from local noon and leaves out your rising sign and houses, which depend on the exact time. You can add a birth time later in Profile.
How do I change the language?
Profile → Language. Nymora is available in English, Turkish, German, Italian, French and Spanish.
My daily reminder doesn't arrive.
Profile → Notifications: turn the reminder on and pick a time. If Nymora asks you to allow notifications, allow them in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nymora.
How do I start over with different details?
Profile → Start over deletes your details on this phone and takes you back to the beginning.

Handful: common questions

When does the new puzzle come out?
At midnight in your time zone. Everyone plays the same word on the same date.
How do I get Handful Pro back on a new phone?
Open Handful, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
My coins or streak didn't move to my new device.
Handful syncs through iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud on both devices, then open Handful on both while online.
What does a streak freeze do?
If you miss a day, a freeze is used automatically so your streak continues. You can hold up to two; Pro players get one free each week.
I ran out of rows. Can I keep going?
Once per game you can add one more row for coins or by watching a short ad (free with Pro). Or reveal the answer and come back tomorrow.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Settings → Ad privacy choices in Handful. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
I was charged but didn't get my coins.
Reopen Handful while online; unfinished purchases are delivered when the app starts. If they still don't appear, write to us with the date of purchase.
